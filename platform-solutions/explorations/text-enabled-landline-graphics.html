What is a text-enabled landline?

A text-enabled landline allows you to send and receive text messages using your existing voice-only phone number. Enabling SMS will not affect your voice calls.

This works through Hosted SMS - text traffic is routed separately from voice, so your number never leaves your existing carrier. Our landline text messaging app works in the background, so enabling SMS will never interrupt your voice calls.

Once a landline number has the ability to send texts and receive them, you will manage the new communication channel with any device that connects to the internet. This can include a mobile device, tablet, desktop computer, or laptop.
